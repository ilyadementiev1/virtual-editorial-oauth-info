Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026.

Virtual Editorial Backup is a private utility operated solely for its owner's server backups. It is not offered to third parties.

Google Drive access

The utility requests the limited drive.file scope. It can create and manage only the Google Drive files and folders created through this application. It does not request access to unrelated Drive content.

Data handling

Backup data is encrypted by Restic before upload and remains in the owner's Google Drive account. The utility does not sell or share data, serve advertising, or use analytics or tracking.

Retention and deletion

Automated retention keeps 14 daily and 12 monthly backup snapshots. Expired application-created backup objects are removed during maintenance. The owner can revoke OAuth access and delete all application-created files at any time.

Contact

Questions can be submitted through the repository issue tracker.
